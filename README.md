2. Project Objectives
Demonstrate the practical application of generative artificial intelligence in supporting student registration.
Explore how an AI-powered chatbot can guide students through registration procedures, explain registration requirements, and provide relevant information.
Improve students' access to registration guidance by offering an interactive tool that can help answer common questions and clarify registration steps.
Document the chatbot's purpose, functionality, and potential benefits for educational institutions, students, and future project development.

6. Example or Demonstration
The chatbot is intended to assist students by providing guidance and answering common questions about the registration process. The following example illustrates how a student might interact with the chatbot.

Sample Prompt:

"I am a new student and need help with the registration process. Can you guide me through the steps and explain the information or documents I may need?"

Expected Example Response:

"Welcome! I can help you understand the student registration process. First, check your institution's admission and registration instructions. Confirm the required documents, prepare your student identification or admission details, and follow the institution's official registration steps. If you are unsure about a requirement or deadline, contact the appropriate admissions or registration office for confirmation."

Demonstration Purpose:

This example illustrates how conversational AI can make registration information easier for students to understand, provide step-by-step guidance, and help address common registration questions.

## 8. Limitations and Access
The chatbot is accessible through Poe using the project link provided in this repository. Access may depend on the availability of the link, Poe's platform requirements, and the chatbot's sharing settings.
The chatbot is designed to provide general guidance on student registration procedures and answer common questions. However, the accuracy and usefulness of its responses may vary. Students should verify registration requirements, deadlines, fees, and official procedures with their respective educational institutions.
This GitHub repository serves as a documentation and reference resource for the project. It does not necessarily contain the chatbot's underlying source code, configuration, or AI model. The chatbot's functionality is subject to the capabilities and limitations of the Poe platform.
